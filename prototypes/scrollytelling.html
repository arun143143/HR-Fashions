How a fitting works

  1. 1

    Measure

    We take your measurements and talk through the design, the fabric and the date you need it.

  2. 2

    Cut and stitch

    The fabric is cut to your numbers and stitched, with lining and finishing done by hand.

  3. 3

    Fit and adjust

    You try it on, we adjust it until it sits right, and then it is yours.
